## The Challenge

**Background:** Lone Mesa Energy is a fictional oil producer operating six sites and eighteen pieces of equipment. Equipment failures, maintenance delays, spare-parts shortages, and safety concerns are disrupting production.

**Your Challenge:** Act as consultants to identify an operational problem and propose one practical business or technology solution that improves efficiency, reduces downtime, or strengthens workplace safety.

Use the provided **Assets, Events, Production, Readings, Solutions, and HSE** datasets to support your recommendation.

### Guiding Questions

1. **What problems should the company prioritize?**  
   Identify the key major operational challenges using relevant data. Consider equipment downtime, production losses, costs, or safety concerns. Which is the highest priority?

2. **What does the data reveal?**  
   Analyze relevant datasets to identify trends, patterns, or inconsistencies. Support your findings with calculations or visualizations.

3. **What solution would you recommend?**  
   Propose an improvement such as equipment monitoring, maintenance scheduling, spare-parts management, or automation. Explain its benefits, estimated costs, and safety considerations.

4. **How would your solution work?**  
   Create a functional prototype or interactive dashboard using the provided data. Demonstrate how employees would use it to support operational decisions. Excel, Power BI, Tableau, Python, or web applications are acceptable.

5. **How would the company implement the solution?**  
   Outline the main implementation steps, resources, training needs, and measures of success or KPIs.

### Deliverables

All teams are required to prepare and present a slide deck of **no more than 10 slides**, highlighting their key findings, data analysis, and proposed solutions. Each team will have a **maximum of 7 minutes** to present. Every team member must actively participate and be prepared to answer questions about their data, methodology, and recommendations.

Teams may also choose to develop a technical prototype or dashboard to support their proposed solution. **Any technical prototype must be submitted alongside the final slide deck.**
