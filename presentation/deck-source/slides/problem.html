AAYAN (about 30 sec) Lone Mesa lost 6,599 hours to unplanned breakdowns last year. When we broke that time down, one thing jumped out: almost a quarter of it, 24%, was a broken machine just sitting there waiting for a spare part to arrive. And one site is worse than the rest. Arroyo, top left on this map, has 40% of all the parts-waiting time and only 42% of its spare shelf stocked. It’s also one of three sites locked by overdue safety actions, the red cages. So we picked one problem: parts waiting. And one priority site: Arroyo.
