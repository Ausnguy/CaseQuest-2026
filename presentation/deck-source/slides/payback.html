Even if everything goes wrong at once, meaning a smaller effect, slow safety sign-off and oil near $50 a barrel, it still pays for itself by month sixteen. The biggest lever is how fast the safety actions get closed, which is exactly why safety comes first. One note: these are modeled margins from the case formula, not booked cash.
