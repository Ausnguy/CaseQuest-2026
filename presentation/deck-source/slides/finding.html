AUSTIN (about 30 sec) This is the chart that decided our recommendation. Each dot is a site. Left to right is how full its spare-parts shelf is. Up and down is how often a breakdown had to wait for a part. They sit almost perfectly on a line. Arroyo’s shelf is 58% empty, and 56% of its breakdowns waited. Juniper’s shelf is nearly full, and only 10% waited. The correlation is −0.995. And when a breakdown does wait, it loses 16 to 18 hours, no matter what broke. Spare parts is the only option that fixes this stage, and it helps with every kind of failure. That’s why we chose it.
