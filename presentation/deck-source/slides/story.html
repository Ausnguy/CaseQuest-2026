AAYAN (about 35 sec) We want you to be able to trace every number we show, so here’s the chain. What we saw in the data: 24% of downtime is waiting for parts. What the case says: a spare-parts program cuts that wait by about 60%, with a range of 35 to 75. That number comes straight from the case’s Solutions sheet, not from us. So we stock spares, and we get about 968 hours of downtime back every year. That’s 666 in the first year while it rolls out. Just as important is what we did NOT claim. We claim no injury prevention, no repair-cost savings, and no hours from sensors or AI. Everything else is recorded data or the case’s own numbers.
