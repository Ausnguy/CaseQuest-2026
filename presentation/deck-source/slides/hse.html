SIDRA (about 25 sec) Last, and most important: safety is never skipped. No override. A site with an open severity-five safety action can’t start, there’s no button to bypass that, and only the safety supervisor can sign off a closure. People decide. The system only gives advice. A person approves every warning and every dispatch, and every decision is logged. Work untouched. We only cut the time spent waiting for parts. Lockout and tagout, gas monitoring and restart steps stay exactly as they are. And if the data ever fails, every site defaults to on hold. Thank you. We’re happy to take your questions.
