SIDRA (about 30 sec) Here’s the twelve-month plan. Month zero, the site safety supervisors start closing the overdue safety actions, the most serious ones first. Month one, we pilot at Canyon, a site that isn’t on hold. The planner and storekeeper audit the shelf and stock the critical kits. Month two, Cedar and Juniper. Month four, Arroyo, our biggest win, but only once its safety actions are verified closed. Then Mesa and Prairie. Training is short, for planners and storekeepers. We judge the pilot on how full the spare shelf gets. If Canyon reaches 90% by month three, we expand. If it doesn’t, we revise. Any safety bypass, and we stop.
