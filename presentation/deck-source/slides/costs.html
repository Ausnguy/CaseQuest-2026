NICK (about 30 sec) We’re recommending one improvement: a critical spare-parts program at all six sites. The total to start is $227,500. That’s $168,000 for the spare parts program and $59,500 to clear the overdue safety actions, which the company needs to do anyway. $33,000 of that safety work is what unlocks the three blocked sites. After that, it runs at $19,500 a year. It fits inside every site’s existing staff hours. Even Arroyo, with only two technicians, uses 44 of its 120 available hours. And based on the case’s own numbers, it gets back about 968 hours of downtime a year. All costs come straight from the case data. We didn’t invent any prices.
