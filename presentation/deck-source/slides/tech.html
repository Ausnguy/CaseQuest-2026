04

WALI (about 30 sec) Here’s what sits behind the demo you just saw. On the left, data the company already has: sensor readings, work orders and the safety register. No new systems. In the middle is Parvius. It cleans and checks the data, flags machines likely to fail, tracks the parts warehouse, and holds any site that isn’t safe. On the right, and this is the important part, people make every decision. The planner stages the parts, the safety supervisor signs off, and the manager sends the engineer. The system won’t send anyone without the right parts, and every decision is logged. The failure model only advises, and we don’t count any savings from it.
